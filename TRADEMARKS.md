# Name and logo

"Polynya" and the Polynya logo belong to Danil Klimov (lyoraeth).

You may:
- share unmodified official releases under the name Polynya;
- say that your fork or module is "based on Polynya" or "for Polynya";
- use the logo in articles, reviews and links to the project.

You may not:
- name a fork or a modified build "Polynya" or use the logo as its icon;
- present a modified build as an official release or yourself as its author or original distributor;
- bundle Polynya with other software in a way that suggests it is part of that software.

Modules may use the name in their description ("a Polynya module").
Questions: open an issue in the repository.
